Bots: privacy policy

"Bots" is a private app used only by its owner, Will Baker, to let his own personal assistant bots read and send email and manage calendar events in his own Google accounts.

Data from Google accounts is processed only on the owner's private server, only for the owner's own requests, and is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored on that server and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
